# Derren Brown and Asi Wind — music research

Checked October 14, 2026. The public sources support a handful of named music leads and several palette observations, not 50 verified atmosphere tracks used onstage. The 60 Derren additions are transparently labeled style recommendations. No production-confirmed recording claim is made.

The 70 downloaded additions are distinct licensed recordings; the references below do not inflate that count. Commercial soundtrack leads are linked for reference without claiming their recording rights. No McFly custom Enigma song is included.

## Source confidence

The Richard Shakeshaft archive says its music identifications were assembled from fan contributions and ear-based identification. A listing is a useful lead, not a production music credit. See https://www.rmjs.co.uk/derrenbrown/tv.php .

- **fan-identification:** Fan archive identification; not a production credit or independently confirmed recording version.
- **audience-report:** An audience member names a song; production-wide use and the recording version remain unconfirmed.
- **audience-palette:** An eyewitness describes the musical sound but does not identify a track.
- **unresolved-guess:** A commenter proposes an identification without corroboration. Do not treat it as established.
- **artist-context:** Artist-hosted or first-person context informs programming; it does not establish a show soundtrack.
