# Magician and mentalist music library

The owner-requested collection is preserved at `C:/dev/personal/show-control-app/music-library/`. The **5 November 2026 all-music Android test APK also contains the complete archive and credits**; installation into the app is deliberate and offline. The collection contains **170 distinct MP3 recordings**, grouped into **20 show roles**, with **326 effect/scene entries**. The recordings total about **10 hours 21 minutes**: the preserved original 100, **60 Derren-inspired additions**, and **10 Asi-inspired additions**. Recordings come from Kevin MacLeod's official [catalog](https://incompetech.com/agent-section/) and Chris Zabriskie's official album player; both composers publish [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) declarations. Zabriskie's [license and credit guide](https://chriszabriskie.com/use/) requires song, artist, and album attribution.

`Research.html`, `Music-Research.md`, and `music-research.json` document twelve sourced observations, their confidence, and their stage/TV context. Audience reports describe vintage swing before Svengali, Get Happy before one Only Human performance, and That's Life at a Showman ending with its recording version unresolved. The fan archive identifies Beethoven's fifth piano concerto's second movement beside a blockhead sequence. These are research leads; **the 60 additions are recommendations, not 60 tracks claimed to be used by Brown**. No bespoke McFly Enigma song is included. The Asi review identifies an intimate setting but no soundtrack; those ten tracks are new effect-specific recommendations. Neither artist nor composer endorsement is claimed.

The three ZIP packages (`Show-Control-Magic-Music-170.zip`, `Show-Control-Magic-Music-100.zip`, and `Show-Control-Atmosphere-Additions-70.zip`) are **not stored in Git** because each exceeds GitHub's 100 MB file limit; `.gitignore` excludes `music-library/*.zip`. The MP3s, artwork, catalog, and credits remain in the repository, and nothing in the repository regenerates the ZIPs, so keep the local copies and distribute them separately, for example as release assets. Normal builds never use them. An all-music Android build (`SHOW_CONTROL_INCLUDE_MUSIC=1`) bundles the 170 package when it is present and verified; when it is absent, the build prints a warning and continues without included music, the app hides **Install included music**, and `tools/build-android-test.ps1` reports `includedMusic: false`. A present archive or recording with a changed checksum still fails the build.

## Use the collection

- Open `Library.html` for local listening, waveform previews, collection/role filters, effect-name and composer search, research links, and credit details. Keep its `mp3` and `artwork` folders beside it. This page works offline and uses one audition player without automatic advancement. Preview plays the complete MP3 at its starting gain; the native cue's trims and fades are listed separately.
- Read `Cue-Guide.md` for entries, fades, silence, volunteer movement, genuine waiting, theatrical suspense, and finales. `catalog.csv` gives a sortable inventory; `CREDITS.md` supplies each recording's attribution, license, ISRC, source, and any changes.
- In the all-music APK, open **Shows**, scroll to **Included music**, and choose **Install included music**. This adds one 170-song show with 20 OMS playlists, retaining existing shows, selection, language and appearance. It shows measured progress and allows cancellation before the final commit. When all 170 recording hashes already exist, it shows that the songs are available instead of offering another installation. No separate download is required. The APK is 1.48 GB; allow several additional GB for archive staging and the installed audio.
- Alternatively, copy `Show-Control-Magic-Music-170.zip` to the phone and choose **More → Settings → Import library backup**. The 1.33 GB package adds one show with 20 OMS playlists. This manual backup restore retains its existing preference-restoration behavior, unlike the included-music installer.
- If the original 100 is already imported, use `Show-Control-Atmosphere-Additions-70.zip` instead. The 609 MB package adds a separate show with seven playlists, preserving the existing show and avoiding another copy of its 100 songs. Importing both new ZIPs would duplicate the additions. Imports apply English/Dark Blue preferences; choose your preferred appearance afterward if needed. They do not start playback.

Every song has credits and cue direction in Notes, an original category cover, a starting trim, measured attenuation, and role-specific fades. Defaults are **1× speed, no delay, manual advance, autoplay off, looping off, crossfade off**, with optional crossfade duration saved as 2 seconds. The complete delivered MP3 remains available for adjusting trims. Four original ambient recordings longer than 15 minutes are six-minute excerpts with a three-second ending fade. Thirty Zabriskie recordings were converted from official AAC to complete 256-kbps MP3; the other 136 MP3 payloads are unchanged. Changes are identified per track. Original covers are CC0; these asset licenses are separate from the application's MIT code license.

The original 100 MP3s, filenames, numbers, and ZIP remain available. The gallery, full 170-song package, and guide refine ten original cue directions and eleven named Asi effect mappings, favoring clear speech and silence at choices/proof. Importing only the additions does not overwrite old song notes. Changed first-edition delivery documents are retained under `editions/100/`. The new CSV also repairs the first edition's middle-dot label encoding defect.

## Verification and limits

`validation-report.json` records acquisition, complete FFmpeg decoding, waveform/loudness analysis, SHA-256 uniqueness, 25 asset edge checks, ten tested combinations, archive checks, native import, and preview evidence. Both archives use the documented [schema-v2 stored ZIP format](library-backup-format.md). The actual app inspector, manifest validator, and compiler validated 20 full-library playlists with 190 payload CRC/SHA checks and seven additions playlists with 77 payload checks. The 170-file hash set is unique, and all original file identities are preserved.

The additions were imported through the Android API 36 emulator's system picker and persisted as 70 songs, 70 app-owned media references, seven playlists, and seven covers. Together with the preserved original show, the native database contains all 170 recording hashes. All three pre-existing show documents remained unchanged. Cold launch remained stopped; native previews of Cylinder One and Cool Vibes started, accepted seeking, and stopped cleanly with zero remaining voices. The emulator has audio output disabled; decoding and transport observations do not prove listening quality. Physical Android/iOS, PA balance, subjective fit, and musical phrase boundaries require rehearsal/device evidence. No cloud AI service, provider key, account, or runtime network is needed.

For live use, retain each used recording's credit in the show's program. MacLeod's [FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html) permits spoken credit where no program exists; Zabriskie's credit includes the album. For recorded shows, include credits in the video description. Ask the relevant composer about paid alternatives when attribution is impractical. The guide offers music direction for the owner's handling; it does not reproduce a named artist's soundtrack or technical method.
